# Diary: Star logo and app icons

The site had dropped the ear logo from its header, but the favicons and web app icons still showed it. The goal was a new logo, a square on a pink background, and icons generated from it.

## Step 1: Find a logo and generate the icons

**Author:** main

### Prompt Context

**Verbatim prompt:** We'll need a logo. Square, pink background. Can you make one? Save in assets/
**Interpretation:** Design a square logo on pink-600 in the site's visual language, and save it under `/assets/`.
**Inferred intent:** A mark for favicons and app icons that fits the pink sky, replacing the ear.

### What I did

The first attempt was the "A" from the constellation wordmark: white lines and stars on pink. Markus: "Unfortunately don't like it at all". A sheet of six concept sketches followed (a single sparkle, level bars, a Bluu Next "A", a note, sound waves, the stacked name), and Markus asked for "just a regular star". The plain geometric five-point star "looks like wordart", and a second sheet of six stylised stars missed too.

Markus then shared a reference image of a hand-cut star, and I traced it. `/assets/logo.svg` is a white five-point star on a `#e60076` square: tilted slightly clockwise, with uneven points (the right and lower-right ones longest), rounded tips, small rounded inner corners, and edges bowed in a little. `/assets/logo.png` is a 1024px render.

From the SVG I rendered `/public/favicon-96x96.png`, `/public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180px), `/public/web-app-manifest-192x192.png` and `/public/web-app-manifest-512x512.png`, and built `/public/favicon.ico` from 16, 32 and 48px renders with Pillow. `/public/favicon.svg` is a copy of the logo. The manifest colours went from `#E50076` to `#e60076`, and the unused ear logo at `/public/images/logo.png` is deleted. `/PRODUCT.md` and `/DESIGN.md` record the logo.

### Why

The icons are the only place the brand shows outside the page, and they were still the ear Markus disliked.

### What worked

Working from a reference Markus brought. Six rounds of my own sketches never landed, and the reference landed on the first trace.

### What didn't work

- The constellation "A" left pink notches where the legs met the sparkle points, the same "cut-off star" effect Markus had already rejected on the site. Gaps between lines and stars fixed the notches, but not the verdict.
- A sheet of sketch previews first rendered without the pink squares. I had replaced `width="512" height="512"` in every SVG to resize the previews, which also stripped the background `rect` of its size.
- No SVG rasteriser was installed (`rsvg-convert not found`, `resvg not found`), so every PNG was rendered by Playwright at its exact size.

### What I learned

Markus's taste for the mark is hand-made and imperfect, not geometric. Uneven points and soft tips carried the character, while extra elements (glints, trails, cut-outs) did not.

### What was tricky

Keeping the maskable icons inside Android's safe zone. The star's tips reach about 32% of the width from the centre, inside the 40% limit.

### What warrants review

`/assets/logo.svg` and the icons in `/public/`. Check the favicon in a browser tab after a hard reload.
